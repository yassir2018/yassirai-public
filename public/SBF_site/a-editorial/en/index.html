An independent Syrian nonprofit serving the public interest, bringing together businesswomen and businessmen, experts, institutions and partners. It mobilizes expertise and resources and builds partnerships that support economic and social development, empower the private sector and strengthen Syrians’ contribution to their country’s future.

Areas of work Why the Forum

01

From humanitarian response to sustainable development solutions.

The Forum was founded in 2012 It was founded on the belief that serving and empowering Syrians is a shared responsibility requiring organized institutional action, effective partnerships and initiatives with tangible impact. Since its establishment, it has supported Syrian communities through development and humanitarian projects that respond to needs and create broader pathways to empowerment and capacity building.

Today, as justice has been achieved and Syria begins a new path toward renewal and reconstruction, the Forum’s role is renewed as an inclusive space and an active platform for developing initiatives, building alliances, connecting resources with needs and expertise with opportunities, and enabling individuals, institutions and communities most in need to take part in recovery and development.

02

Expertise became dispersed and ties weakened. A platform was needed to reconnect them.

Recent years brought profound changes to Syria’s economic and social environment, dispersing expertise and capital, weakening links between the business community and development opportunities, and increasing the need for an institutional platform able to unite efforts, build partnerships and lead initiatives with lasting impact.

The Forum was therefore created as an inclusive space bringing together businesswomen and businessmen, institutions, experts, donors and development partners within an institutional framework founded on cooperation, governance, transparency and collective action.

Areas of work

The Forum works from a vision that supports investment, strengthens institutions and expands the private sector’s role in development and the rebuilding of Syria’s economy. Its work focuses on four main pillars.

  • 01

    Business and economy

    Developing companies and business leaders, strengthening trade and investment partnerships, supporting small and medium-sized enterprises, organizing economic conferences and forums, and improving the investment environment.

    Pillar details →
  • 02

    Development and empowerment

    Vocational training, employment, entrepreneurship, economic empowerment, local development and corporate social responsibility—programs that build skills, improve livelihoods and create jobs.

    Pillar details →
  • 03

    Knowledge and policy

    Economic studies, policy papers, market analysis, institutional governance, and development and sector indicators—to support decision-making and improve the business environment.

    Pillar details →
  • 04

    Media and economic advocacy

    Media campaigns, success stories, economic awareness, institutional content and media relations—to build a positive image of Syria’s economy and make policy easier to understand.

    Pillar details →

Strategic priorities

The Forum will focus on six main priorities in the next phase.

  1. 01 Rebuild the network of Syrian businesswomen and businessmen.
  2. 02 Develop a platform for partnerships and investment opportunities.
  3. 03 Support small and medium-sized enterprises and entrepreneurship.
  4. 04 Produce economic studies and policy work.
  5. 05 Strengthen the private sector’s social responsibility.
  6. 06 Build regional and international partnerships that serve Syria’s economy.
