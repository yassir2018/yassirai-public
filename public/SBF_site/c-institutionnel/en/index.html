Who we are

The Forum is a platform that combines economic and development expertise and connects the private sector with development, reconstruction and social-responsibility programs.

Vision
A prosperous Syrian economy led by a responsible private sector, effective institutions and strategic partnerships that advance sustainable development and build Syria’s future.
Mission
To mobilize the expertise and capabilities of Syrian businesswomen and businessmen, build effective economic and development partnerships, connect resources with opportunities, and empower institutions and individuals through high-quality initiatives and programs supporting economic development, entrepreneurship, investment, employment and capacity building.
Values
Integrity and transparency · Professionalism · Partnership and complementarity · Innovation · Social responsibility · Sustainability.
