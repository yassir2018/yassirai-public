Supporting education and building the future

Challenge
Thousands of Syrian children have been affected by displacement and reduced access to education.
Intervention
Delivering education programs, supporting schools and securing learning environments in partnership with other organizations.
Results
Continuity of education, improved quality of educational services, and support for thousands of students and families.

Economic empowerment

Challenge
High unemployment and limited income opportunities for families.
Intervention
Delivering vocational training, supporting small businesses and economically empowering participants.
Results
Job creation, establishment of small businesses and improved incomes.

Humanitarian response

Challenge
Disasters and humanitarian crises faced by Syrian communities, including the response to the 2023 earthquake.
Intervention
Mobilizing resources, coordinating with partners and delivering relief and urgent-support programs.
Results
Services reached the people most in need, partner coordination improved and response times accelerated.
