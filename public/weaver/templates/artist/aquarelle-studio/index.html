About

"Water has a memory. Every brushstroke carries the spirit of the moment it was made."

I'm a watercolor artist based in the south of France, painting full-time for 12 years. My work celebrates the unpredictability of the medium — each painting is a collaboration between intention and the beautiful chaos of water.

My pieces are held in private collections across Europe, Japan, and the United States.
