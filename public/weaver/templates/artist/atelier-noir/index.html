Artist Statement

"I don't paint to show. I paint to destroy something comfortable inside myself."

Working from a studio in Berlin since 2008, my practice investigates the physicality of paint as both material and language.

Each painting begins as an act of aggression — scraping, pouring, wiping away. What remains is a record of the struggle between intention and accident. I work exclusively at large scale, where the body becomes part of the process.

Collections: MoMA, Tate, Centre Pompidou, Fondation Beyeler.
