About the Artist

"I don't paint what I see — I paint what the light whispers."

Trained at the Florence Academy of Art and the Royal Academy in London, I've dedicated two decades to the study of light and its relationship with the human experience.

My work draws from the Dutch Masters — Vermeer, Rembrandt, de Hooch — reinterpreting their luminous vision through a contemporary lens. Each painting is built through dozens of transparent glazes over months of patient observation.

Currently based in Amsterdam, I work from a canal-side atelier where the ever-changing Northern light becomes my most essential collaborator.

— Eleonore V. Hartmann
