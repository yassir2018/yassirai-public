Biography

About

Born 1978, Lyon, France. Lives and works between Paris and New York. Recipient of the Marcel Duchamp Prize (2023) and the Pritzker Fellowship (2021).

My practice is rooted in subtraction — removing the unnecessary until what remains reveals something essential about how we inhabit and perceive space. I work with architects, engineers, and light designers to create environments that alter the visitor's relationship to the built world.
