About

"Sculpture is the art of subtracting everything that is not the idea."

Working primarily in bronze, marble, and steel, I explore the tension between solid form and empty space. Each piece begins as a conversation with the raw material.

My studio is located in Pietrasanta, Italy — the city of sculptors — where I've worked for over two decades alongside master artisans. My installations span public spaces from Paris to Tokyo.
