Post-Session

AFTERCARE

🧴

Moisturize

Apply a thin layer of unscented moisturizer 3× daily for the first two weeks.

🚿

Clean Gently

Wash with lukewarm water and mild soap. Pat dry — never rub the area.

☀️

Avoid Sun

Keep your new tattoo out of direct sunlight for at least 4 weeks. Use SPF 50+ after healing.
