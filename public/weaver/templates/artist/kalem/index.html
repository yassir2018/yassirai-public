About

"In every stroke lies a breath. Calligraphy is not writing — it is listening to the ink."

Trained in classical Arabic, Japanese, and Western calligraphy traditions, I've spent 15 years perfecting the dialogue between hand, tool, and paper.

My work bridges ancient discipline with contemporary expression, creating pieces for collectors, brands, weddings, and meditation spaces worldwide.
