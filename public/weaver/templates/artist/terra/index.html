Collections

Our Work

Collection I

Everyday Ritual

Functional stoneware for daily use. Mugs, bowls, and plates designed to bring warmth and intention to every meal. Handthrown on the wheel, glazed with our signature earth-tone palette.

Collection II

Sculptural Vessels

One-of-a-kind sculptural pieces that blur the line between function and form. Inspired by organic shapes — shells, seed pods, and geological formations.

Collection III

Wabi-Sabi

Embracing imperfection. Raku-fired pieces with unpredictable crackle glazes, each one uniquely marked by the kiln's breath. No two alike.
