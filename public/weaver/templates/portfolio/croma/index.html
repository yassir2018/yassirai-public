A little about me.

I'm a self-taught illustrator and brand designer from Barcelona, now based in Amsterdam. For the past 7 years, I've been helping startups, publishers, and cultural institutions bring their visions to life with colorful, emotionally engaging artwork.

My work has been featured in Communication Arts, It's Nice That, and Adobe's Creative Cloud showcase. When I'm not drawing, I'm probably at a flea market or eating tapas.
