The Visionary

Olivier Marchand

Executive Chef & Founder

Trained in the kitchens of Alain Ducasse and René Redzepi, Chef Marchand brings two decades of global culinary mastery to Zenith. His philosophy is rooted in the pursuit of purity — letting the natural essence of each ingredient tell its own story on the plate.

"Cooking at this level is not about adding — it's about removing everything that doesn't belong."
